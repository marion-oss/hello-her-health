Concept A
Chunky Sanctuary
Translates the box-format reference into the Sanctuary palette. Chunky Surface cards, huge italic Cormorant display, fuchsia signal at low frequency, Ember warmth in radial sheens. Decisive, almost editorial-poster.
Concept B
Iridescent Sanctuary
Brings the soft glassmorphic AI-assistant aesthetic onto Void canvas. Surfaces are frosted with subtle ember/fuchsia gradients underneath. Lighter, dreamier, less committed than A. Best for evening / quiet moments.
Concept C
Pure Brand Deck
Faithful to the PDF: Void canvas, generous space, italic Cormorant copy, the breathing-form ellipse low and right, fuchsia used only as a 6–8px dot and one arrow. Quietest of the three; most editorial.
